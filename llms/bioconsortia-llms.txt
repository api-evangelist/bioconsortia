# BioConsortia

> BioConsortia, Inc. is an agricultural biotechnology company in Davis, California (with laboratories in
> New Zealand) that discovers and commercializes microbial biologicals — nitrogen-fixation biofertilizers,
> bionematicides and biofungicides, and biostimulants — largely delivered as seed treatments for row crops
> and specialty agriculture. BioConsortia publishes **no public API, developer portal, SDK, or
> machine-readable specification**; its RhizoViz and GenePro platforms are internal laboratory R&D
> instruments, not products offered to developers.

## Platform (internal R&D instruments — not developer products)

- [Our Platform](https://www.bioconsortia.com/our-platform): Overview of the discovery and optimization stack.
- [Advanced Microbial Selection (AMS)](https://www.bioconsortia.com/our-platform/advanced-microbial-selection): Patented directed-selection process for identifying beneficial microbial consortia.
- [RhizoViz](https://www.bioconsortia.com/our-platform/rhizoviz): Visualization and measurement tool for plant-associated microbial colonization under varying soil pH and temperature.
- [GenePro](https://www.bioconsortia.com/our-platform/genepro): Genomics and gene-engineering platform used to design and optimize microbial strains.
- [Pipeline](https://www.bioconsortia.com/our-platform/pipeline): Products moving toward registration.

## Products

- [Our Products](https://www.bioconsortia.com/our-products): Full biological product portfolio.
- [Nitrogen Fixation](https://www.bioconsortia.com/our-products/nitrogen-fixation): Microbial biofertilizers that displace synthetic nitrogen in cereals, vegetables and row crops.
- [Biocontrol](https://www.bioconsortia.com/our-products/biocontrol): Bionematicide and biofungicide seed treatments.
- [Biostimulants](https://www.bioconsortia.com/our-products/biostimulants): Nutrient-use-efficiency and yield products.

## API surface

BioConsortia has no public API surface. Probed 2026-08-07 and all returned HTTP 404 on
`https://www.bioconsortia.com`: `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`,
`/redoc`, `/api`, `/v1/openapi.json`, `/graphql`, `/llms.txt`, `/.well-known/security.txt`,
`/.well-known/agent-card.json`, `/.well-known/agent.json`, `/.well-known/api-catalog`,
`/.well-known/openid-configuration`, `/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server`,
`/.well-known/ai-plugin.json`, `/developers`, `/developer`, `/api-documentation`. The hosts
`api.`, `developer.`, `docs.`, `app.`, `portal.`, `status.` and `trust.bioconsortia.com` do not resolve.
